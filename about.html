About us

The Arkand story

A local care company built on a simple belief: everyone deserves kindness, good company, and the comfort of their own home.

Why we started

Arkand Care began with something most of us have felt: worry about someone we love. A parent managing on their own. Days that feel long and quiet. Little jobs around the house that slowly become big ones.

We saw that what many older people need first isn't complicated or clinical — it's a friendly, dependable person who turns up when they say they will, shares a laugh over a cup of tea, and quietly helps with the everyday things.

So that's what we built: a local home-care company for Hackney, Ealing and Barnet where warmth comes first, and where every visit is delivered with dignity.

“Write the way you'd speak to a kind neighbour you respect — and care the same way too.”

— The thought behind everything we do

Being straight with you

Where we are with CQC registration

We believe trust starts with honesty, so here is exactly where we stand:

We're fully insured, and a registered company with policies built to care-sector standards. We're completing our CQC registration so we can offer personal care soon. Right now we provide companionship and home help, which don't require CQC registration.

That means today we can help with company, conversation, outings, meals, light housework, shopping and errands — and once our CQC registration is complete, we'll be able to offer personal care in Ealing and Barnet too. If that's something you'd like when it's available, you're very welcome to register your interest and we'll let you know as soon as it's ready.
