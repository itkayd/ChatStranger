Our services

Help that fits around your life

Companionship and help at home across North-East Redbridge — from a 30-minute visit to a regular weekly routine.

Available now

Companionship

Loneliness can be harder than any household chore. Our companionship visits bring conversation, laughter and a familiar friendly face — someone who's there because they genuinely enjoy your company.

  • Friendly company and conversation
  • A cup of tea and a chat
  • Hobbies, games, and shared activities
  • Accompanied walks and outings
  • Trips to appointments or the shops

Help at home

The little jobs that keep a home comfortable can quietly pile up. We'll take care of them — cheerfully, and just the way you like things done.

  • Light cleaning and tidying
  • Laundry and ironing
  • Shopping and collecting prescriptions
  • Preparing meals and snacks
  • Everyday errands

Respite for family carers

Caring for someone you love is a labour of love — and it's tiring. We can step in with friendly, companionship-based sitting so you can rest, run errands, or simply have an afternoon to yourself, knowing they're in kind company. (Sitting company only — we don't provide personal care during respite visits.)

Coming soon — subject to CQC registration

Personal care and specialist support

We're currently completing our registration with the Care Quality Commission (CQC). Once that's done, we plan to offer the following regulated services across North-East Redbridge. These are not available yet — but if you'd like them in future, we'd love to hear from you now.

  • Personal care (washing, bathing, dressing)
  • Dementia support
  • Medication support
  • Night & overnight care
  • Hospital discharge support
