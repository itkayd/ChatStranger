Do work that genuinely matters

We're looking for kind, reliable people across North-East Redbridge who'd love to bring good company and a helping hand to older neighbours.

The kind of person we're looking for

You don't need years of care experience to join Arkand Care — you need a warm heart, patience, and the reliability to turn up when someone is counting on you. If you're the sort of person neighbours trust and friends describe as "genuinely kind", we'd love to hear from you.

  • Naturally kind, patient, and a good listener
  • Reliable and punctual — people will depend on you
  • Happy helping around the house and out and about
  • Local to North-East Redbridge
  • Experience is welcome, but character comes first

“The best part of this work is simple: you leave someone's home knowing their day was better because you were in it.”

— The Arkand way of thinking about care
