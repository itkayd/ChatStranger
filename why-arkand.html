Why Arkand

Reasons families trust us

Inviting someone into your home — or your mum's or dad's home — is a big decision. Here's why families across London and nearby areas feel comfortable choosing Arkand Care.

💚

Kind & reliable local team

We hire for kindness first, and we're local to the areas we serve — so you're welcoming a neighbour, not a stranger from across town.

😊

Familiar faces you can trust

Wherever possible you'll see the same companion each visit. Familiarity builds trust — and often a real friendship.

🗓️

Flexible visits to suit you

From a 30-minute pop-in to a full afternoon, weekly or more often — visits fit around your routine, not ours.

🌿

Dignity, always

Your home, your habits, your choices. We listen first, help gently, and never rush. It's your life — we just make it a little easier.

📄

No long contracts

Clear, simple invoices and no tie-ins. If your needs change, so do we — simple as that.

🛡️

Honest & properly set up

We're fully insured and a registered company, with policies built to care-sector standards — and we're completing our CQC registration so we can offer personal care soon.

The heart of it

We treat your home like a privilege

Every visit begins at your doorstep — and we never forget what it means to be welcomed across it. That's the standard we hold ourselves to: the care and respect we'd want for our own family, in our own home.
